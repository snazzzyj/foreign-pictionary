Rules & Setup

🎯 Objective

Guess as many foreign language words as possible within 60 seconds. Most points after 12 rounds wins!

👥 Roles

  • 🖊️ Drawer: Sees this Host Screen. Draws the English prompt without talking, numbers, or letters!
  • 🤔 Guesser: Looks at their physical 100-word sheet and says the foreign word aloud.

🏗️ Structure

12 rounds split into 3 language blocks of 4 rounds each (🇸🇪 Swedish ➔ 🇨🇳 Mandarin ➔ 🇮🇩 Indonesian). Alternate drawer/guesser roles each round.

📌 Key Rules

❌ No speaking, mouthing, writing letters/numbers, or pointing at sheets.
✅ Pronunciation just needs to be close enough!
